HIDANGAN KHAS

Nasi Jamblang

Nasi Jamblang is a traditional dish from Cirebon, West Java. It consists of steamed rice wrapped in teak leaves and is usually served with various side dishes, such as fried chicken, tofu, tempeh, and sambal. The teak leaf wrapping gives the rice a distinctive aroma and helps keep it fresh.

Main ingredients
Rice, various side dishes with traditional seasonings, and teak leaves for wrapping.
Taste
Savory and slightly salty.
Recommended places
Nasi Jamblang Iis, Nasi Jamblang Brekele.

Empal Gentong

Empal Gentong is a traditional Cirebon dish made from beef and cooked in a rich, flavorful coconut milk broth. It is traditionally cooked in a clay pot called a gentong, which gives the dish its distinctive name. It is usually served with rice or lontong and topped with chives and fried shallots.

Main ingredients
Beef and yellow coconut milk broth.
Taste
Savory and slightly salty.
Recommended places
Empal Gentong H. Apud, Empal Gentong Krucuk.

Docang

Docang is a traditional Cirebon dish made with lontong, cassava leaves, bean sprouts, and grated coconut, served with a savory fermented soybean broth. It is usually topped with fried onions and traditional crackers, creating a unique combination of fresh, savory, and slightly sour flavors.

Main ingredients
Lontong, cassava leaves, fermented soybean broth, and traditional crackers.
Taste
Savory and slightly salty.
Recommended place
Docang Pasar Perum, usually sold by street vendors.

LITTLE STORY ABOUT

Nasi Jamblang

Fun Fact: Nasi Jamblang was originally created as an affordable meal for workers building the Great Mosque of Cirebon. The rice is traditionally wrapped in teak leaves, which help keep it fresh and give it a distinctive aroma.

Empal Gentong

Fun Fact: The name Empal Gentong comes from the traditional clay pot (gentong) used to cook the dish. Some traditional vendors still use firewood and clay pots to preserve its authentic flavor.

Docang

Fun Fact: Docang is considered one of Cirebon's traditional breakfast dishes. Its unique broth contains fermented soybean paste called oncom, giving it a distinctive savory and slightly tangy flavor.
